# Objective

AI video generation for TV show episode summaries.

## Goal

Produce AI-generated recap videos that summarize episodes of different TV shows and serials.

## Specifications

| Parameter        | Value                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Characters**   | Multiple per video        |
| **Video Length**  | 1 – 1.5 minutes (up to ~60 sec per episode) |
| **Quality**      | Decent / cinematic        |
| **Content Type** | Episode summaries / recaps |

## Content Focus

- Each episode of a TV show → one recap video (~60 seconds)
- Multiple characters must appear and remain consistent across shots
- Scalable across different shows and seasons
